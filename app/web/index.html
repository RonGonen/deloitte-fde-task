How the score works

Forecast growth (FAA TAF 10-yr CAGR), recent momentum (FAA YoY + TAF), capacity pressure (ops per runway, slot status, forecast vs peak, delays and NAS share, cancellations) and scale (log enplanements). Ask “how is the expansion score calculated?” for the full method.

Every number comes from a named public source (FAA, BTS, OurAirports). Scores are screening percentiles, not investment advice. Open Sources & caveats under each answer.
